Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 5, 2026

At CampusConnect, we are committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This policy outlines how we collect, store, and responsibly handle data within the Punjab Technical University (PTU) ecosystem.

1. PTU Ecosystem & Data Visibility

CampusConnect is designed exclusively for networking and professional collaboration within Punjab Technical University and affiliated colleges. Directory profiles, project listings, and public posts are visible to verified students, alumni, and recruiters inside the ecosystem to facilitate career growth. We do not sell or leak directory search indices to public search engines (like Google or Bing).

2. College Email Verification

To preserve network integrity and prevent unauthorized access, we require college email verification (e.g., student domains affiliated with PTU like `.ptu.ac.in`) during registration. We store this verification status to confirm your affiliation, ensuring that our safe, professional environment remains reserved for authentic members of the campus community.

3. User-Generated Content (UGC)

You retain ownership of the data you post. This includes your profile details, hackathon/project collaboration listings, post updates, and peer-to-peer messages. Messages sent securely between users are encrypted in transit and stored safely in our Supabase database, accessible only by the sender and receiver.

4. Responsible Data Handling

  • No Third-Party Ads: CampusConnect is ad-free. We do not share your profiles or activity history with external marketing networks.
  • Local API Keys: If you configure a Gemini API Key to talk with our chatbot assistant, it is saved securely inside your browser's local storage (`localStorage`) and never transmitted to our backend.
  • Safe Infrastructure: All structured profile details, ratings, testimonials, and contact submissions are stored securely in Supabase backend systems.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, data deletion requests, or our security protocols, please feel free to reach out directly through our Contact Page or email us at support@campusconnect.ptu.ac.in.
